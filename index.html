QuantumFrequencies – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy covers the "QuantumFrequencies" application: a private tool used only by the owner of the QuantumFrequencies YouTube channel to upload and manage that channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API and to read that channel's own YouTube Analytics. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests these permissions for the owner's own channel only:

  • Upload videos and manage their metadata (youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl)
  • Read channel and video information (youtube.readonly)
  • Read the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly)

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to upload, schedule, localize and audit the channel's own videos and to measure their performance.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, with file permissions restricted to that user. They are never shared or sold.
  • No data about viewers or other YouTube users is collected, and nothing is shared with third parties.
  • Metadata text (titles and descriptions) may be sent to Google's Gemini models to translate it into other languages.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
